About Me

Engineer. Automator.
Lifelong Learner.

I'm Chayan Deokar, a Openshift / DevOps Engineer at RedHat, working on building reliable, scalable systems for global connectivity. I'm a 2x Red Hat Certified engineer with a strong pull toward DevOps and MLOps — I like automating the parts of engineering that shouldn't have to be done by hand.

My background spans full-stack development with the MERN stack, Salesforce development, and infrastructure work with Terraform, OpenShift, and Big Data tools like Hadoop. I enjoy sitting at the intersection of application code and the infrastructure that runs it.

Outside of my day job, I run DevOps Insights, a YouTube channel where I break down DevOps tools, tutorials, and real interview experiences — because I believe the best way to learn something is to teach it to someone else.
